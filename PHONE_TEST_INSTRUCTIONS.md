# DocErena v5.0 Phone-Testing Instructions (Windows)

Please refer to the full guide in `review_package/01_APK_AND_SIGNATURE/PHONE_TESTING_GUIDE.md` for complete details.

### Quick Start (Installing on Your Phone)

1. **Copy APK to Phone**:
   - Plug your phone into Windows PC with USB.
   - On phone: Select **File Transfer** (MTP) mode.
   - On PC: Copy `d:\camscanner\docerena_v5_phonetest.apk` to phone's **Download** folder.
2. **Install**:
   - On phone: Open **Files** app -> **Downloads** -> tap `docerena_v5_phonetest.apk`.
   - Allow "Install unknown apps" if prompted, then tap **Install** (or **Update**).
3. **Verify**:
   - Camera scanning, Real Crop with corner pins, Magic Color enhancement, Real Folders, Offline OCR, and PDF Protection all work 100% offline.
